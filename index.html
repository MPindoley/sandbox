How we work together

  1. The first conversation

    Complimentary and without obligation: where you stand, where you want to go, and whether we are the right fit.

  2. Discovery

    Listening first: your goals, your values, your whole financial picture, and what wealth means to you.

  3. Analysis

    Every account, policy and document gathered and measured, so we can see where you stand, the gaps and the risks.

  4. Strategy

    One written plan connecting investments, taxes, insurance, retirement income and estate, in the order that matters.

  5. Partnership

    Then the long part: regular reviews, rebalancing and changes as your life, the markets and the law move.

  6. Passing it on

    Beneficiaries, trusts and the conversations that let what you built reach the people you choose.

Questions

Are you a fiduciary?

On advisory accounts, yes. I am held to a fiduciary standard and act in your best interest, and every cost and potential conflict is disclosed before you decide.

Where are my assets held?

At a regulated third-party custodian, never with me. I hold discretionary management authority, but your assets remain in your name at a regulated institution at all times.

What happens in the first conversation?

It is complimentary, with no commitment: we talk about where you are, where you want to go, and whether working together makes sense for you.

Who do you work with?

Individuals and families at every stage, from the first steps of building wealth to passing it on. As an SE-AWMA®, I am also trained in the particular needs of athletes and entertainers.

Can you work with me where I live?

I work with residents of Ohio, Florida, Michigan, Illinois, Pennsylvania, Texas, South Carolina and Nevada.
